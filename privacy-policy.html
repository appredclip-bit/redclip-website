Redclip Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 7 October 2026

1. Introduction

Redclip (“Redclip”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy, confidentiality and security of information entrusted to our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how Redclip collects, uses, processes, stores, transfers, discloses, retains and protects personal data when individuals and organisations access or use our websites, software, applications, artificial intelligence systems, platforms and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

Redclip provides an AI powered operating platform designed for corporate service providers, professional firms, registered agents, accounting firms, compliance professionals and other businesses involved in corporate administration and professional services.

Depending on the Services subscribed to, Redclip may provide functionality relating to entity management, CRM and workflow management, KYC and compliance, document automation, statutory records, payments, marketplace services, artificial intelligence and related professional workflows.

Because our customers may use Redclip to manage confidential corporate, KYC and professional information, data protection and information security form an important part of how Redclip designs and operates its Services.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy may apply to information concerning:

  • Redclip customers and account holders
  • authorised users and employees of customers
  • clients of corporate service providers and professional firms
  • directors and company officers
  • shareholders
  • beneficial owners and ultimate beneficial owners (“UBOs”)
  • authorised representatives and agents
  • counterparties and business contacts
  • prospective customers
  • website visitors
  • individuals identified within documents uploaded to Redclip
  • other individuals whose personal data is processed through the Services.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with Redclip’s Terms of Service, applicable Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) and any additional terms governing particular Services.

3. Our Role in Processing Personal Data

Redclip may process personal data in different capacities depending on the circumstances.

Redclip Acting for Its Own Purposes

Redclip may determine how personal data is processed in connection with matters such as:

  • account administration
  • subscriptions and billing
  • customer support
  • product administration
  • security
  • fraud prevention
  • communications
  • service analytics
  • legal and regulatory compliance.

Where applicable, Redclip will process such information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Redclip Processing Customer Data

Where a customer uploads, stores or otherwise processes information concerning its own clients or other individuals through Redclip, the customer generally determines why that information is being processed.

In these circumstances, Redclip generally processes the information on behalf of the customer and in accordance with the customer’s instructions, applicable contractual arrangements and applicable law.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary authority, notices, consents or other lawful grounds required to collect and provide such information to Redclip.

Redclip does not assume the customer’s professional, fiduciary, statutory or regulatory obligations merely because the customer uses the Services.

4. Customer Data Ownership

As between Redclip and the customer, customers retain their rights in information, documents and records submitted to the Services (“Customer Data”), subject to applicable law and third party rights.

Customers do not transfer ownership of Customer Data to Redclip merely by using the Services.

Customers grant Redclip only such rights as are reasonably necessary to host, process, transmit, secure, back up and otherwise handle Customer Data for the purposes of providing, maintaining and supporting the Services and performing our contractual and legal obligations.

Redclip does not sell Customer Data or personal data.

9. AI and Customer Data

Redclip recognises that Customer Data may contain confidential and highly sensitive business and personal information.

Redclip will not sell Customer Data.

Redclip will not use confidential Customer Data, identification documents, KYC records or customer uploaded documents to train publicly available general purpose AI models without appropriate authorisation.

Where Customer Data is processed using third party artificial intelligence or technology providers, Redclip will seek to implement appropriate contractual, technical and organisational safeguards consistent with the nature of the processing.

Where technically and legally appropriate, Redclip may use aggregated, anonymised or de identified information that does not reasonably identify a customer or individual for purposes including service improvement, security, analytics and product development.

Redclip maintains internal controls intended to reduce the risk of Customer Data being disclosed between unrelated customer environments.

10. Customer Environment and Data Segregation

Redclip is designed as a multi customer platform.

We implement technical and organisational controls intended to maintain appropriate separation between customer environments and restrict access according to authorised permissions.

Customer Data belonging to one customer should not be intentionally made available to another unrelated customer except:

  • where authorised by the relevant customer
  • through functionality intentionally designed for authorised information sharing
  • where the parties are legitimately connected through a transaction or workflow and appropriate authority exists
  • where required by applicable law.

Redclip applies access controls and other safeguards intended to prevent unauthorised cross customer access.

AI generated risk ratings, screening results, alerts, recommendations and other outputs are intended to assist the customer’s review.

Customers remain responsible for their final professional and regulatory decisions, including:

Redclip assists compliance professionals. It does not replace professional judgement, statutory responsibilities or regulatory obligations.

32. Our Privacy Principle

Redclip is being developed to manage information that may be commercially confidential, professionally sensitive and important to our customers’ regulatory responsibilities.

Our approach is therefore straightforward:

Collect only what is reasonably required.

Use information only for legitimate and authorised purposes.

Protect information with appropriate safeguards.

Maintain appropriate separation between customers.

Give customers reasonable control over their information.

Use AI to assist professionals, not to remove professional responsibility.

Do not sell Customer Data.

Do not treat customer trust as a secondary consideration.

Privacy, confidentiality and information security are fundamental components of the Redclip platform.
